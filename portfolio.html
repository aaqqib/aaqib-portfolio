B A Barry Group

Visit Live Site
  • B A Barry is a Dubai-based FMCG and commodities trading company (DMCC free zone) supplying bulk sugar and cocoa powder to B2B buyers globally. Brought in to diagnose a traffic drop and build a long-term SEO foundation.
  • Identified a February 2026 site outage (5xx errors) as the root cause of a 38–47% traffic collapse. Conducted a full GSC and GA4 analysis, audited 14 pages sitewide for weak or truncated title tags and meta descriptions, and delivered optimised copy for all 14 — client implemented immediately.
  • Performed deep landing page audits for three core product pages (ICUMSA 45 Sugar, Cocoa Powder, Organic Sugar) — diagnosing intent mismatches, CTR killers, missing content sections, and structural issues. Delivered new titles, metas, H1s, and CMS-ready body copy across all three pages.
  • Identified 113 crawlable 404 pages, missing robots.txt, duplicate canonicals, and asset file errors wasting crawl budget. Conducted a full keyword gap analysis, identified 30 net-new blog opportunities across 5 content clusters, and wrote 4 SEO blogs targeting high-intent commercial keywords.
  • Result: Organic sessions grew 44% month-on-month (322 → 465), organic overtook Direct as the #1 traffic channel for the first time, and average search position improved from 16.4 to 7.7.
  • By July 2026 (6-month performance data), organic traffic recovered post-outage, achieving 1.88K clicks and 158K impressions on Google Search Console with an average position of 9.6 and a 1.2% CTR.
B2B FMCG · Technical SEO · Recovery Audit · Content Strategy

Kashmir Travel Guide

Visit Live Site
  • A personal project built from the ground up — from niche research and tech stack selection to content architecture and backend development.
  • Mapped out topical clusters targeting a wide range of Kashmir travel keywords, set up GSC, GA4, and Google Tag Manager, and built a custom backend that pushes content changes live instantly.
  • Integrated an automated weekly SEO blog posting autopilot loop using Gemini 2.5 Flash and GitHub Actions that automatically analyzes existing posts to detect content gaps, drafts 1500+ word articles, auto-injects internal anchor links, and pre-resolves cover images on push.

  • The site is designed for long-term monetisation through AdSense, guest posts, and link selling — leveraging local Kashmir knowledge as a content advantage that generic travel sites can't replicate. The organic growth has turned the site into a valuable lead-generation and monetisation asset, attracting regular inquiries for local tours and guest partnerships.
